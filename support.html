Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All scanning happens on your device. Your photos never leave your phone.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Cleanup Wizard, tap the gear icon, open the upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Cleanup Wizard, and tap Cancel Subscription.
